01 · Global sourcing

Origination matched to your specification.

Crude oil and refined products from producers and refiners in established supply regions. We work back from your specification, volume and timing to the right source, rather than forward from whatever cargo happens to be available.

  • Crude grades and refined products from established suppliers
  • Specification, quality and origin agreed up front
  • Counterparty due diligence on every source
02 · Long-term contracts

Multi-year supply, structured to hold.

The long contract is our discipline. We structure multi-year supply so that your volumes, pricing and delivery windows hold when the spot market doesn’t, and we back it with term arrangements of our own with producers, refiners, storage and shipping partners.

  • Multi-year term supply agreements
  • Fixed, formula or hybrid pricing
  • Volume tolerances and delivery windows agreed up front
  • Credit and performance assurances on both sides
03 · Storage & shipping

Storage and shipping, under one plan.

We arrange tankage, chartering and freight so that product moves from source to destination under one plan: storage close to demand, and vessels ready when the window opens.

  • Tankage and terminal capacity at key hubs
  • Vessel chartering, spot and time
  • Freight, laytime and demurrage management
  • Cargo inspection and documentation
04 · Delivery

Landed where and when it was contracted.

A contract is only as good as the discharge. We manage the last stretch — scheduling, documentation and certification — so what arrives at your terminal is the volume and quality you agreed, in the window you agreed.

  • Discharge scheduling and berth coordination
  • Customs, documentation and compliance
  • Independent quality and quantity certification
  • Post-delivery reconciliation and reporting
Across the vertical

Term arrangements at every stage of the chain.

Reliability is a set of relationships held together end to end. That is why we contract across the whole vertical rather than at one link in it.

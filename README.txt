SILVERTIDE WEBSITE

Open index.html for English or index.es.html for Spanish. Each of the five pages has an EN / ES switch linking to the same page in the other language. Spanish uses professional Latin American terminology for physical energy supply and maritime logistics.
The website ZIP includes the current English and Spanish pages and supporting assets. Earlier design concepts remain in the working folder and are excluded from the deployment ZIP.

All images, fonts for English and Spanish, and JavaScript dependencies are packaged locally. For best navigation compatibility, serve this directory using any static web server.
The original export uses a client-side component runtime; modern browsers are required.
Confirmed company details: Silver Tide Global Inc; Unit 2, 2900 John Street, Markham, Ontario L3R 5G3, Canada; info@silvertideglobal.com; +1-416-764-5706. Business hours have not been supplied.
The English and Spanish Contact forms submit to Formspree using native fetch and HTML POST. Direct email links remain unchanged. See README.md for setup and testing details.
The Careers page describes general areas of interest and accepts career enquiries by email. These are not advertised current vacancies.
Open Silvertide-Content-Next-Steps.txt for remaining owner-supplied content, SPANISH-TRANSLATION-NOTES.txt for terminology decisions, and MOBILE-AND-LANGUAGE-QA.txt for validation details.
The nine photos were created using the built-in Image Generation tool; exact prompts are in image-prompts.json. They depict generic industry scenes and illustrative people.

The homepage uses the completed 45-second industry film (assets/hero-silvertide-45s.mp4) as a muted looping background, with Pause/Play film and a still-image fallback. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see the still until they choose Play film. Main.dc.html and index.html show the same homepage.

Video: eight Runway-generated industry shots, edited to exactly 45 seconds, H.264, 1280 x 720, 24 fps, without audio, optimized for web startup. hero-silvertide-45s-poster.jpg is its first frame. All scenes are illustrative; they do not document company-owned assets or actual employees. The earlier supplied 21.04-second clip remains packaged as assets/hero-tanker-web.mp4.

The responsive layout adapts hero heights to content, keeps the footer wordmark within its column, enlarges mobile touch targets and stacks the supply-chain diagram on smaller screens. Press and media enquiries have been removed. Button hover and keyboard-focus colors use matched contrasting text/background pairs.
